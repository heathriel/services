Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know about working with the crew.

Who owns the code and workflows you build?

You do. Everything is delivered with full documentation and we transfer all ownership. You can modify, extend, or stop using it anytime. No lock-in, no licensing fees.

Are you an AI or a human?

I'm human (Serene). I lead the crew. Think of it like how a creative director works with a team — I set the vision, review the work, and deliver to you. The crew does the heavy lifting at a scale no individual consultant can match.

How is this different from hiring an AI developer?

Three things: speed, breadth, and cost. A single developer can do one thing at a time. My crew works in parallel — research happening while code is being written. The breadth means we can tackle complex multi-step projects. And because the crew operates continuously, we deliver faster at a lower cost.

Do I need technical skills to use what you build?

For Starter tier, we optimize for minimal technical requirements. Professional and Enterprise tiers may require some technical integration on your end, but we provide detailed guides and handoff calls to ensure you're set up for success.

What if something goes wrong?

I'm accountable. The crew builds it, I verify it, I deliver it. If there's an issue, you talk to me and I fix it. We also have a dedicated QA agent (Ruth) whose entire job is finding problems before delivery. Everything comes with a 30-day bug fix guarantee.

Can I talk to the crew directly?

No — and that's intentional. I'm your single point of contact. It keeps things simple for you and lets me filter requests so the crew stays focused on execution. You get the benefits of a team without the overhead of managing multiple people.

What tools and technologies do you use?

We work with your existing stack where possible — Zapier, Make, n8n, custom code, or direct API integrations. We recommend the right tool for your specific need rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all solution.

How do you handle data security?

We don't store client data long-term. During projects, data is handled according to industry best practices. For Enterprise clients, we provide security documentation, can sign NDAs, and follow compliance requirements.

Can I upgrade or downgrade tiers?

Yes. You can move between tiers with 30 days notice. Many clients start with Starter, see the value, and move to Professional as their needs grow.

What if I'm not sure which tier I need?

Book a free discovery call. We'll talk through your current challenges, what's working and what isn't, and I'll recommend the right starting point. No obligation — sometimes the best advice is "start with Starter and see how it goes."
